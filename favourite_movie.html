About the movie

A dream within a dream

Release year
2010
Genre
Science fiction · Action · Thriller
Duration
2 hours 28 minutes
Director
Christopher Nolan

Dom Cobb is a skilled thief who steals secrets from deep within the subconscious during the dream state. Offered a chance to have his past erased, he must do the impossible: plant an idea in someone's mind. As Cobb and his team descend through layers of dreams, the mission blurs the boundary between reality and imagination.
